Rift ARPG privacy policy

Rift ARPG is a test build of a browser game. It has no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share any data.

What it stores

Your save (characters, items and settings) is kept in your browser's storage on your device.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game asks only for the drive.appdata permission. It uses that to keep one save file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your save follows you between devices. The game can't see any of your other Drive files, and it doesn't ask for your name, email or contacts. The save is sent straight from your browser to Google. There's no game server in between.

Sign out under Settings → Google sync. To delete the save, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rift ARPG → Delete hidden app data.

Co-op

When you join a friend's game, your player name, moves and gear are sent to the other players in that room.
